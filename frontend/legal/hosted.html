Version 2 · hosted beta seats only

1. This PawPoller runs on someone else's computer

Most people run PawPoller on their own computer or their own server. This copy is different. It runs on a computer at the home of the person who invited you (we'll call them the operator), and you reach it through your browser. It's yours to use: your own account, your own password and your own data, kept apart from every other seat on that computer.

2. What the operator can and can't see

When you connect a site (FurAffinity, Bluesky and so on), PawPoller has to keep that login so it can post and read for you. On this seat those logins are stored encrypted, with a key that belongs to this seat alone.

  • The operator controls the computer this runs on, so they could in principle reach your logins and your work. Only connect sites if you trust them with that.
  • The operator doesn't see your PawPoller password. It's stored scrambled, the same way as on any install.
  • If you'd rather nobody else could ever reach your logins, run PawPoller on your own computer instead. Everything you make here can move there (see section 5).

3. It's a beta

This is a test of hosting PawPoller for other people. A seat may be paused, moved or wiped. You'll be told first wherever that's possible, but it might not always be: a power cut or a broken computer gives no warning.

The seat shares one home internet connection with the other seats. If a site limits or blocks that connection, every seat on it is affected. PawPoller shows that honestly as an error on that site; it isn't something you did.

4. Backups are on the same computer only

Your seat is backed up every night, and two weeks of backups are kept. Those backups are on the same computer, on a second drive. They protect against a mistake or one failed drive. They don't protect against the whole computer being lost, stolen or burnt.

Keep your own copy of anything you can't lose. Settings → Data → Download everything gives you all of it in one file, any time.

5. You can leave whenever you like

  • Download everything (Settings → Data) gets every piece, story, setting and stat. It opens on any other PawPoller.
  • To take your site logins too, make a moving file (Settings → Moving to another PawPoller). It locks them with a phrase only you choose, and the other PawPoller opens them with it. A plain download doesn't carry them: they're locked to this seat's key.
  • To have the seat removed, ask the operator. Your data is kept for a little while in case you change your mind, unless you ask for it to be deleted straight away. Backups then run out within 14 days.

6. Never sold or shared

Nothing on this seat is sold, shared or used for advertising, and nothing is used to train AI. The Terms of use and Privacy policy above still apply in full. This page only adds what's different about a hosted seat.
