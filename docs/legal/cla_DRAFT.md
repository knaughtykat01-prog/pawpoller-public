**Version 1** · Effective ⟦date⟧

This agreement is between **you** (the person contributing) and **the PawPoller Project** ("the Project"). It
covers everything you submit to PawPoller: code, documentation, translations, artwork, tests and anything else
("your contributions").

## The short version

- **You keep the copyright** in your contributions. You can still use them however you like.
- You give the Project permission to use them, including in the **open-source AGPL release** and in the
  **Project's own paid, hosted version of PawPoller**.
- The Project promises your contributions will **always also be available as open source**.
- You confirm the work is yours to give.

## 1. What you keep

You keep all copyright and other rights in your contributions. Nothing here transfers ownership to the Project.

## 2. The licence you give

For each contribution, you give the Project, and anyone who receives software from the Project, a **worldwide,
royalty-free, non-exclusive, perpetual and irrevocable** licence to:

- use, copy, change, combine and distribute it;
- publish it under the GNU Affero General Public License version 3, or any later version;
- **also** offer it under other licence terms, including the terms of a paid or hosted service, and grant
  others the right to do the same (sublicense).

## 3. Patents

If any patent you own or control covers your contribution, you give the Project, and anyone who receives
software from the Project, a worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive, perpetual and irrevocable licence under that
patent to make, use, sell and distribute your contribution as part of PawPoller. This patent licence ends for
anyone who sues claiming that PawPoller, or your contribution, infringes a patent.

## 4. The Project's promise to you

Whenever the Project distributes your contribution, it will **also** make it available under the AGPL-3.0 or
another licence approved by the Open Source Initiative. Your work can't be taken only into a closed product.

## 5. What you confirm

When you submit a contribution, you confirm that:

1. it's your own original work, or you have the right to submit it under this agreement;
2. if you made it as part of a job, your employer has agreed to you contributing it under this agreement, or has
   waived its rights;
3. you've told us about any part that comes from someone else, including its licence;
4. you've told us if any meaningful part was produced by an AI tool, and you have the right to contribute it;
5. you are **18 or over**, or a parent or guardian has agreed to this agreement for you.

## 6. Moral rights

Where the law lets you, you agree not to assert moral rights in a way that would stop the Project using your
contribution as this agreement allows. The Project will still credit contributors in the source history.

## 7. No obligations

The Project doesn't have to use your contribution. You give it as it is, without any warranty, unless you agree
otherwise in writing.

## 8. Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of **New South Wales, Australia**.

## 9. How to agree

⟦Decision owed: either a bot on GitHub (CLA Assistant) that asks on each new contributor's first pull request,
or a comment on the pull request: "I have read the PawPoller CLA version 1 and I agree to it."⟧ The Project keeps
a record of who agreed, when, and to which version.

## 10. Changes

If this agreement changes, it gets a new version number. What you agreed to applies to the contributions you
made under it.
