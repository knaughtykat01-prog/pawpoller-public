# PawPoller roadmap

What we're working on, and what comes after it, in the order we expect to build it. It changes as we learn;
tell us what you'd like in #ideas.

Written for testers. Each item keeps its backlog ID in a comment so the roadmap script can track it; the
backlog itself stays private. Plain words only, no tester names, nothing that isn't decided.

## Now

- **A writing desk for stories.** Write, plan and revise in one place: a calm page to write on, a corkboard of your scenes, and side-by-side revising with snapshots of earlier drafts. Being designed now; your story file stays exactly as it is. <!-- id: WRITEDESK -->
- **This roadmap.** What we're working on, kept up to date here with every release. <!-- id: ROADMAPDC -->

## Next

- **Writing tools in every text box.** Look up a word's meaning, similar words and opposites, spot a word you've used too often, and see how close you are to each site's length limit. Works offline, no AI. <!-- id: WRITEASSIST -->
- **Styled descriptions.** Bold, italics, links and lists written once, shown properly on every site in the way that site supports, with a preview per site. <!-- id: DESCSTYLE -->
- **A setup guide for each way of installing.** One short guide for the desktop app, one for Docker, one for hosted seats, so you only read what applies to you. <!-- id: SETUPSPLIT -->
- **Piczel.** Post to Piczel alongside your other sites. <!-- id: PICZEL -->
- **A better shelved view in the Library.** <!-- id: LIBSHELF -->

## Later

- **More writing sites: Royal Road, Scribble Hub and Ream.** <!-- id: WRITESITES -->
- **Patreon, SubscribeStar and Ko-fi.** Post to the sites your supporters follow you on. <!-- id: MONETIZE -->
- **Music and video sites.** Post and track audio and video, such as YouTube and SoundCloud. <!-- id: MEDIAPLATS -->
- **Your own Overview.** Choose which numbers and charts sit on your first screen. <!-- id: AC -->
- **Short video walkthroughs.** From first start-up to your first post, a minute or two each. <!-- id: TUTORIALS -->
- **Leaderboards, if you want them.** Off unless you turn them on, like levels and medals. <!-- id: XPLEVELS -->

## Recently shipped

- **4.87** · Every site says how your post went, even after you close the page. Setup remembers where you got to, and post forms list the sites you're signed in to first.
- **4.86.0** · A Start screen that asks what you came to do, and scheduling a story's chapters in three steps with a calendar.
- **4.85.0** · Levels, medals and rewards, off unless you turn them on.
